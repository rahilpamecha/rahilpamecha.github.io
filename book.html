The Novel

Of All The Times

Literary Romance  ·  Written by Rahil Pamecha

Ethan Hartley has spent his life building something no one can take from him — a career, a certainty, a fortress assembled brick by careful brick from the ruins of his father's collapse. Love, he decided at twelve years old, is simply the most efficient way to lose something.

Nora Calloway arrived early to everything, because she understood earlier than most that tomorrow was not guaranteed. She loved the way her mother had taught her — fully, without hiding, with her whole heart still in front of her.

They were, by every account that matters, made for each other. They were also, by that same account, never quite going to find each other at the same time.

"This is not a story about two people who didn't love each other. It is a story about two people who loved each other completely, and simply never once managed to do it on the same day."

Of All The Times by Rahil Pamecha moves across years, across other loves, across a silence that grows slowly between two people who keep finding each other at exactly the wrong moment — until the moment arrives that cannot be walked away from.

From the Prologue

A story about the grief of almost.

There is a moment that happens before every love story begins — a moment nobody marks, because nobody knows yet that it matters. It looks like nothing. A hallway. A glance held one second too long. A name said by someone else in passing, filed away without ceremony.

It is only afterward, when the story has already finished happening, that you go back and find it: that was the place everything turned. That was the first thread.

He did not believe in love so much as he believed in proof. She did not believe in proof so much as she believed in presence. They found each other somewhere in the middle of those two truths, and for a while, they built something neither of them could name.

Then he looked away. And by the time he looked back, the world had moved on without him.

Of all the times. Of all the chances.
Of all the ways a person can be standing right next to everything they need,
and still manage to walk away from it.

About the Author

Rahil Pamecha

Author of "Of All The Times" & Software Professional

Rahil Pamecha writes with a quiet intensity, capturing the ache of unspoken words, one-sided love, and the beauty found in heartbreak.

With a minimalist voice and emotional depth, stories by Rahil Pamecha explore the fragile spaces between connection and solitude — the silences between people who should have said more, and the distances between those who felt too much to speak.

Alongside creative writing, Rahil Pamecha is a software professional based in Pune, India, with a background in engineering and enterprise technology at Barclays.
